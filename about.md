# <cp>LEVELTUNE IMPORTER</c> <cy>— CUSTOM MUSIC WORKFLOW</c>

<cg>**YOUR EDITOR. YOUR AUDIO. YOUR LEVEL.**</c>

LevelTune Importer is an early Geode editor extension concept for managing custom audio used in Geometry Dash levels.

## <cy>▰ EDITOR ACCESS</c>
Open the editor and tap the LevelTune music button to access the custom music helper.

## <cp>▰ AUTHORIZED AUDIO SOURCES</c>
The intended workflow is to accept a direct audio-file URL from a source that permits downloads, or a locally selected audio file, then validate the format and store it for the game.

## <cj>▰ IMPORTANT ABOUT YOUTUBE</c>
A YouTube watch URL is not a direct MP3/OGG file. This project will not bypass YouTube's download restrictions or extract copyrighted audio without permission. Use music you created, have permission to use, or sources that explicitly provide downloadable audio.

## <cr>▰ DEVELOPMENT STATUS</c>
This package currently contains an editor-button prototype and an explanatory dialog. It does **not yet download audio or register an imported song**. A real implementation still needs a tested Android file/network workflow, audio-format validation, storage management, and integration with Geometry Dash's custom-song system.

## <cg>DESIGNED FOR GEODE</c>
Target: Geometry Dash 2.2 Android 64-bit with Geode. The source must be compiled and tested against the target SDK before installation.
